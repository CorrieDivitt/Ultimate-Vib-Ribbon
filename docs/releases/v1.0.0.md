The first release of Ultimate Vib-Ribbon: a free, fan-made version of Vib-Ribbon for PC VR, the Meta Quest 3, Windows monitors and (experimentally) Android phones. It plays the original's songs and courses from **your own disc**, and turns your own music into courses.

Not affiliated with Sony Interactive Entertainment or NanaOn-Sha.

Downloads:

- **Windows, monitor and PC VR** (`Ultimate-Vib-Ribbon-Windows-1.0.0.zip`, portable): `Vib-Ribbon Launcher.exe` starts either the VR version (PC VR through OpenXR: Meta Quest Link, Virtual Desktop or SteamVR) or the flat monitor version, with display settings and the controls shown.
- **Meta Quest 3** (`Vib-Ribbon-VR-Quest-1.0.0.zip`: the APK to sideload, with QUEST.txt): the standalone VR version.
- **Android phones and tablets** (`Vib-Ribbon-Android-1.0.0-experimental.zip`: the APK, with ANDROID.txt), **experimental, feedback welcome**: the flat version with on-screen buttons (obstacle shapes or the PlayStation buttons) or a gamepad, sideways only. The disc image is imported in the game by picking the folder that holds it. It has been tried on few devices so far: please report what phone or tablet you used and what worked or did not.

All versions need your own copy of the PAL disc (SCES-02873) as a disc image (a .cue and its .bin files); nothing of the game is included.

Highlights:

- An in-game options menu on every screen (Start on a gamepad, P or Tab on the keyboard), with the camera moved by the analog sticks everywhere.
- Camera beside Vibri (the default on Windows and in VR) or the original's; Original look restores the original's settings.
- Difficulty Easy, Normal, Hard and Extreme (an obstacle on nearly every beat) for the disc's songs and your own music, **experimental, feedback welcome** (Normal is the original's courses exactly; high scores kept at Normal).
- Scroll speed changed mid-song with L2/R2 (Page Up/Page Down on the keyboard).
- Exit on the title menu.
- Your own music on the Quest from its Music folder; on a phone, the disc imported by picking its folder.
- On the Quest, a clear message instead of a black screen when the disc image is missing.

### Installing

- **Windows:** unzip anywhere and run `Vib-Ribbon Launcher.exe` (Windows may warn about an unknown program the first time: More info > Run anyway). The zip's README.txt has the controls.
- **Quest 3:** see QUEST.txt in the zip (SideQuest, or adb step by step).
- **Android:** see ANDROID.txt in the zip.

If you installed a test build from before 1.0 on a Quest or phone, uninstall it first: 1.0 is signed with the release key, and later versions will install over it.

SHA-256 checksums of the three zips are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`. Problems and feedback: please open an issue with your version, device and log (see the README).
