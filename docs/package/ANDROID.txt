Vib-Ribbon on Android phones and tablets
https://github.com/CorrieDivitt/Vib-Ribbon-VR

EXPERIMENTAL: the phone and tablet version is new and has been tried on few devices.
Feedback is welcome (what phone, what worked, what did not), as an issue on the page
above.

The game is an APK you install yourself. It contains no part of the game: it plays your
own copy of the PAL disc (SCES-02873), from a disc image (a .cue file and its .bin
files) that the game copies in once. A 64-bit phone or tablet with Vulkan (most from
2019 on) is needed.

INSTALLING
1. Copy the APK (app-flat-debug.apk) to the phone, open it in the phone's Files app
   and allow installing from that app when asked. (Or with adb on a PC:
   adb install -r app-flat-debug.apk)
2. Put your disc image's .cue and .bin files in a folder of their own on the phone, for
   example Download/Vib-Ribbon (copy them from a PC over USB). Android does not let
   apps use the Download folder itself, so the files need their own folder.
3. Start Vib-Ribbon. It asks for the disc image: tap Pick folder and choose that folder
   (then "Use this folder" and Allow). It copies the .cue and its .bin files in once;
   after that you may delete your copies. Pick files is there too (press and hold one
   file, then tap the others). Options > Change disc imports another.

PLAYING
The screen turns sideways. Two buttons for each thumb:
  left:  Block above, Pit below          right: Loop above, Wave below
  Pairs: press both at once.
Options > Touch buttons shows them as the obstacles' shapes or as the original's
PlayStation buttons (L1, Down, R1, X).
- The button at the top right opens the options menu (in a song it pauses).
- In the menus: tap to choose, swipe to move, the arrow at the top left goes back
  (so does Android's back).
- The camera (Options > Visual > Camera > Beside Vibri, the starting one): drag across
  the screen away from the buttons to go round Vibri; pinch to zoom.
- A Bluetooth gamepad works too; the touch buttons hide while it is used.
- Exit is on the title's menu.

YOUR OWN MUSIC
Put MP3 or FLAC files in the phone's Music folder. The first time you open the music
picker the game asks whether it may read your music: allow it.

PROBLEMS
With adb on a PC: adb logcat -d -s vibplay:* > phone-log.txt

THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt (inside the APK and beside this file) lists the libraries the
game is built from and their licences.
