Vib-Ribbon VR (fan project, free, not affiliated with Sony or NanaOn-Sha)
https://github.com/CorrieDivitt/Vib-Ribbon-VR

This download has no game in it. It plays your own copy of the PAL disc
(SCES-02873), from a disc image you made of it: a .cue file with its .bin files.

THE TWO PROGRAMS
  Vib-Ribbon.exe       on your monitor
  Vib-Ribbon VR.exe    in a PC headset, through its OpenXR runtime (Virtual Desktop,
                       Meta Quest Link or SteamVR: start that first)
Make shortcuts to either on your desktop or Start menu if you like (right click,
Send to > Desktop). Nothing needs installing; to remove the game, delete its folder.

THE FIRST TIME
The game asks for your disc image: choose the .cue file. It is remembered; Start >
Change disc picks another.

CONTROLS (keyboard / gamepad)
  Block        Left arrow, A         left shoulder or trigger
  Pit          Up or Down arrow      d-pad up or down
  Loop         Right arrow, D        right shoulder or trigger
  Wave         Space, J, K           top or bottom face button
  Settings     P or Tab              Start (in play it pauses)
  Menus        arrows, Enter, Esc    d-pad, A, B; the mouse clicks and scrolls
  Fullscreen   F11 or Alt+Enter
  Performance  F3                    left stick click
With Settings > Visual > Camera set to Beside Vibri, the right stick, the mouse wheel
or Ctrl and the arrows zoom, and the left stick, a right-button drag or Ctrl and the
arrows go round her; Home or the right stick click puts the view back.

YOUR SAVE FILE AND LOG
  %APPDATA%\VibRibbonVR\VibRibbonVR\save.txt   high scores and settings (both programs)
  %APPDATA%\VibRibbonVR\VibRibbonVR\log.txt    what the monitor program printed (log-vr.txt: the headset's)
Send the log with any problem report.

The Quest 3 standalone version is a separate download (an APK to sideload).
