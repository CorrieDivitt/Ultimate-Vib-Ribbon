Vib-Ribbon VR (fan project, free, not affiliated with Sony or NanaOn-Sha)
https://github.com/CorrieDivitt/Ultimate-Vib-Ribbon

This download has no game in it. It plays your own copy of the PAL disc
(SCES-02873), from a disc image you made of it: a .cue file with its .bin files.

START HERE: Vib-Ribbon Launcher.exe
  Choose your disc image (.cue), the monitor or a VR headset, and the display
  settings (window or fullscreen, resolution, refresh rate, VSync, frame limit),
  read the controls, then Play. Nothing needs installing; to remove the game,
  delete its folder. Windows may warn about an unknown program the first time:
  More info > Run anyway.

THE PROGRAMS (the launcher starts them; they also start on their own)
  Vib-Ribbon.exe       on your monitor
  Vib-Ribbon VR.exe    in a PC headset, through its OpenXR runtime (Virtual Desktop,
                       Meta Quest Link or SteamVR: start that first)

IN THE GAME
  The options menu is on Start (gamepad) or P / Tab (keyboard), on every screen; in
  a song it pauses. Most of the settings are there: the camera, colours, scroll
  speed, timing feedback, glow, calibration, the original look and more.
  Exit is on the title's menu (B jumps to it).

CONTROLS (keyboard / gamepad)
  Block        Left arrow, A         left shoulder (LB)
  Pit          Up or Down arrow      d-pad up or down
  Loop         Right arrow, D        right shoulder (RB)
  Wave         Space, J, K           A or Y
  Options      P or Tab              Start
  Scroll speed Page Up / Page Down   triggers (L2 / R2), also during a song
  Menus        arrows, Enter, Esc    d-pad, A, B; the mouse clicks and scrolls
  Fullscreen   F11 or Alt+Enter
  Performance  F3                    left stick click
With Options > Visual > Camera set to Beside Vibri: the right stick, the mouse wheel
or Ctrl and the arrows zoom; the left stick, a right-button drag or Ctrl and the
arrows go round her; Home or the right stick click puts the view back.

YOUR SAVE FILE AND LOG
  %APPDATA%\VibRibbonVR\VibRibbonVR\save.txt   high scores and settings (all three programs)
  %APPDATA%\VibRibbonVR\VibRibbonVR\log.txt    what the monitor program printed (log-vr.txt: the headset's)
Please send the log with any problem report.
